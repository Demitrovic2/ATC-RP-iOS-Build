Install with SideStore

1
Initial SideStore setupUse a computer one time to install SideStore and pair the iPhone.
2
Trust the developer appOn iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → trust the Apple Account used for SideStore. Enable Developer Mode when requested.
3
Download ATC RPTap the red Download button above and save the IPA to Files.
4
Open it in SideStoreIn SideStore, add/install the ATC RP IPA and let SideStore sign it with your Apple Account.
5
Refresh before expiryWith a free Apple Account, SideStore shows a 7-day expiry counter. Refresh the app before it expires.
Official SideStore Install Guide Pairing File Help
iOS 26.4+: if SideStore reports pairing/minimuxer issues, its official troubleshooting guide recommends updating to a SideStore nightly build.
Important: do not share your Apple Account password with ATC RP staff or anyone else. Signing should happen on the member's own device/SideStore setup.
